## Editorial decisions

- Introduce a **scoped specialized diagnostic page** in FR and EN. This does NOT add a fifteenth product to the P1 registry; it is a service variant within the existing diagnostic portfolio.
- Preserve original comprehensive `/offre/` / `/en/offer/`, including the general six-stage knowledge/observation framework and its separately quoted documentary proposal.
- Publish the five-step specialized execution-boundary method **as a proposal**, not as a completed customer project or an automatically executed test campaign.
- Historical proposals are deliberately preserved as provenance: the specialist pack suggested **1–3 weeks**, while the existing general documentary offer contemplated **2–3 consultant days over a week**. Neither estimate has enough completed client-mission evidence to become a public commitment. The **specialized public page now quotes time and fees only after scoping**, without a numerical duration. Each engagement needs a written quote and agreed evidence/test access.
- Keep X-Ray, REG Prevent/Prepare, REG+ and TENIR-Gov as distinct assets with registry-tracked versions/statuses.
- Reuse only the already published **fictional** banking demonstration; never imply real transaction effect or privileged customer access.
- Surface four homepage actions: request a specialized scoped diagnostic, try simulations, review bounded published software, explore TENIR/REG framework.
- The apparently duplicated TENIR narrative from `home.md` is **already inside a collapsed HTML `details.reading-note`** on the homepage; do not remove it based solely on text extraction.
- Do not upload the full commercial pack, prequalification questionnaire, client report template or outreach emails to the public website. They are working documents and could be mistaken for established engagements.
