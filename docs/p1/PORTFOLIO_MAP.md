## Rules of interpretation

- **TENIR** is the wider conceptual framework. **REG** is its specialized execution-governance/requirements/evidence work. **TENIR Labs** is the connected portfolio and outward-facing environment. None means "one monolithic platform".
- A **client offer** is not automatically a deployed software product; an **instrument** is not automatically an admitted REG rule; a **demo** is not an operational guarantee; a **technical foundation** is not a client-facing service.
- A customer's first action can be a journey, a direct need, a request for source material, or a discussion of a runtime pilot. **Guided Governance Diagnostic** is the immediate quoted offer, not a compulsory funnel.
- Every public entry must present (1) pain / question, (2) function, (3) bounded output, (4) maturity and deployment, (5) explicit limitations. Preserve declared / tested / demonstrated / deployed as separate evidence classes.
- "Owner pending" is an **unfilled decision**; do not interpret skiredj-prog repository ownership as confirmed product, legal, risk or normative authority.
- Runtime status below is tagged-source-level. Reachability and content of https://tenirlabs.cc/ and https://mobile.tenirlabs.cc/discover could not be independently confirmed in this review.

### P1-02 — Guided Governance Diagnostic | immediate commercial offer

- **Pain / starting question:** A bounded agent action, incident or proposed change needs a defensible next decision.
- **Function:** Scope the question, select relevant Prevent/Prepare work and instruments, examine source material with a client, distinguish facts and unknowns.
- **Output:** Sourced diagnostic sheet / synthesis, controls and evidence gaps, limited prioritized next steps, human restitution under an agreed quote.
- **Maturity / deployment:** **Offer described on public-site main**, offered on a scoped-and-quoted basis; first real engagements, scope/load and customer validation **not evidenced as completed**.
- **Limitations / non-claims:** No certification, automatic implementation, production action, unbounded legal assessment or guaranteed conclusion. A client need not begin here.
- **Relationships:** Can orchestrate P1-03/P1-04 and one or more P1-05/P1-06/P1-07. May lead to a separately scoped P1-08 technical pilot, never auto-admission.
- **Source of truth:** [FR offer](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/fr/offre.md), [EN offer](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/en/offer.md); commercial terms are per accepted scope/quote.
- **Owner:** Website/source custodian skiredj-prog; **commercial offer owner / delivery decision authority pending designation**.

### P1-03 — REG Prevent | requirements-to-controls approach

- **Pain / starting question:** A consequential action may reach authorization or commitment before its conditions are understood.
- **Function:** From stated requirements to candidate controls; investigate authority, reversibility, duplicate execution, uncertain results, constraints and assumptions.
- **Output:** Candidate control map / Prevention Brief; clarified requirements, critical assumptions, gaps and next checks.
- **Maturity / deployment:** REG v0.1 **working-draft approach**; bounded prototype/pilot workflow, not an independently verified production product or completed client deployment.
- **Limitations / non-claims:** Candidate controls are neither admitted invariants nor binding runtime policy; no automatic compliance finding. Not a mandatory initial stage.
- **Relationships:** Can begin from P1-07 Translation or direct requirements; complements P1-04 Prepare and, after explicit authorization/verification, may inform P1-08 configuration.
- **Source of truth:** [REG product framing FR](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/fr/produits-et-services.md), [REG v0.1 working draft FR](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/fr/publications/reg-v0.1.md).
- **Owner:** Site/source custodian skiredj-prog; **method owner pending designation**.

### P1-04 — REG Prepare | evidence-readiness approach

- **Pain / starting question:** Which claims can available records support before or after a consequential action?
- **Function:** Delimit scope; inventory records, controls, responsibility, provenance, dates and gaps; distinguish declared, observed and independently tested facts.
- **Output:** Evidence Readiness Pack / traceable evidence register, supported-and-unsupported claim matrix, unknowns and verification plan.
- **Maturity / deployment:** REG v0.1 **working-draft approach**; pilot workflow under development/testing; no general client readiness proven.
- **Limitations / non-claims:** Presence of a file, hash or log is not proof of its truth, compliant behavior or external effect. Neither an automatic certification nor necessarily post-execution.
- **Relationships:** Complements P1-03; can incorporate P1-05/P1-06 discovery findings and records from P1-08 only after provenance/quality checks; conformance results remain scoped.
- **Source of truth:** [REG product framing FR](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/fr/produits-et-services.md), [REG v0.1 working draft FR](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/fr/publications/reg-v0.1.md).
- **Owner:** Site/source custodian skiredj-prog; **method/evidence qualification owner pending designation**.

## Two distinct entry modes — preserve architecture, no redesign in P1

| Dimension | Journey-led — Recognize my situation | Need-led — Solve this particular problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question | Who am I, and what situation do I recognize? | Which bounded problem must I address? |
| Existing site anchor | [src/data/journeys.ts](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/data/journeys.ts): five journeys | [FR Products & Services](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/fr/produits-et-services.md) / [EN](https://github.com/skiredj-prog/skiredj-prog.github.io/blob/main/src/content/en/products-and-services.md): future problem-to-route index |
| Route | Context → examination → bounded output → human decision | Pain → relevant instrument / offer / source → maturity and limits → next step |
| Examples | Discover TENIR, delegate, design an agent, investigate an unknown effect, inspect technical sources | Exposure → X-Ray; hidden assumptions → Discovery; requirements → Translation/Prevent; evidence gap → Prepare; runtime boundary → TENIR-Gov |
| Permitted destinations | Offer, product, source, simulation, pilot discussion, contact, explicit insufficient evidence | Same destinations, selected **by need**, not as a forced diagnostic |
| State on website main | Journey pages exist; needs menu lacks product catalog entry | Catalog page exists, but direct need-to-product differentiation is incomplete |
