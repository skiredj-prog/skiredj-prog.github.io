## The gap, read both ways

<p><strong>Top down:</strong> what leadership expects and what is written, but that nothing demonstrates at the moment the agent acts.</p>

<p><strong>Bottom up:</strong> what the agent can do, but that no one has ever admitted.</p>

<p>The seven levels read for one decision: business strategy, AI ambition, organization, process, infrastructure, value and assurance.</p>

## Your maturity tier

<p>Six tiers, always in the same order. A tier is reached when its exit condition is demonstrated, not when it is written down.</p>

<div class="table-scroll">
<table class="fit">
  <thead><tr><th>Tier</th><th>Exit condition</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>0 · Defined</td><td>What the agent may do is written down, and an owner is named.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>1 · Observable</td><td>Every action is linked, in the log, to its request and its authorization.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>2 · Controlled</td><td>An out-of-bounds action is blocked before execution, and the block has been tested.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>3 · Proven</td><td>A third party can verify that what was tested is what runs.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>4 · Held</td><td>The setup has withstood exercises and real incidents.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>5 · Coordinated</td><td>Several agents share resources under written arbitration rules.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>

<p>The plan always targets the next tier, never two at once: you cannot control what you cannot yet see. The same scale applies to every scope, which makes two plans comparable.</p>

<p>The free self-assessment gives a first reading, based on your answers. The week establishes the demonstrated tier, on evidence.</p>

## The week

<p>The mode is set at scoping. <strong>Exploratory</strong> if no agent acts yet: the week says what will need to be demonstrated first. <strong>Engaged</strong> if an agent is in pilot or in production: the week says what is demonstrated today.</p>

## The instruments

<p>The method is <a href="/en/standard/">REG</a>: <a href="/en/portfolio/prevent/">REG Prevent</a> reads the gap top down, <a href="/en/portfolio/prepare/">REG Prepare</a> bottom up. <a href="/en/portfolio/gov/">TENIR-Gov</a>, our open-source software, can be one of the means in the plan, from tier 2 onwards. It is never a prerequisite. During the week, we use the <a href="/en/portfolio/workbench/">Workbench</a> to configure the review and to read the evidence back.</p>

## What it is not

<p>Not a certification, not a legal opinion, not a compliance opinion. The week does not touch your production. Decisions remain with your own owners.</p>
