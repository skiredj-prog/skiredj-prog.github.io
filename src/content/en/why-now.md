## The shift

For a long time, an AI error remained an answer error. A person still read, checked and decided.

Now a system can call a tool, change a record, commit a spend, send an instruction or participate in an operational decision. The output no longer stays inside a conversation: it enters a chain of action.

The market presents this as productivity. The missing question is simpler: **who answers for the consequence when several actors made the action possible?**

## Four shifts to see

- **From answer to action** — an output becomes a command, promise or state change.
- **From tool to delegate** — an organization entrusts a capability to a system.
- **From local control to a chain of actors** — vendors, platforms, integrators and teams share the path.
- **From visible error to dispersed responsibility** — everyone can explain their part, no one the whole.

## The question the market postpones

> **The vendor promises. The integrator connects. The organization delegates. The person bears the effect. Who can answer?**
