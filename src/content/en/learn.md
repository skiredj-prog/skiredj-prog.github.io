## Intent is not authorization

A legitimate objective does not automatically grant the right to use every tool, dataset or method.

## Authorization is not safety

A technically permitted action may still be wrong for the live context, timing, recipient or consequence.

## Simulation is not reality

A successful controlled scenario does not prove behavior under latency, concurrency, interruption or real dependencies.

## Supervision is not effective control

A human in the loop is not a control if the action is faster than the person’s ability to intervene.

## Traceability is not responsibility

A log can show that an event happened. It does not always show who could authorize it, stop it or repair its consequences.

## The evidence chain

```text
Rule → authority → configuration → action → effect → observed state → recovery or repair decision
```

A conclusion may be positive, partial, pending or not demonstrated. **“We do not know yet”** is a valid conclusion when the uncertainty is located and followed by a next verification.
