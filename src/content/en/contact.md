<h2>Cercle Inverse, apart</h2>
<p>Cercle Inverse — Vision &amp; Structure Document v1.1, by the same author — describes an intellectual and human ecosystem: crossing complexity, crises, and reconstructions. Its mission is written there on three axes: rebuild, clarify, transmit. Its pillars — anchoring, Project Phoenix, Strategist-Architect, cycle — and its Studio and Foundation entities are not TENIR components, and they are not repositories on this map. The genesis narrative kept by the prior rendering line crosses the name at the moment when “Phoenix” is no longer enough. That crossing explains an origin. It does not import the vision into the canon, nor into KB3.</p>

<h2>An independent specification</h2>
<p>“Independent architecture — Value trajectories and passage control”, specification v2 of 2 October 2026, says so in its opening: it depends neither on a TENIR-Gov engine, nor on a score formula, nor on CES states, nor on a ledger type. Its object is a value trajectory across authority domains, and a passage as the decision unit at the boundary. In its own vocabulary it replaces “Realm” with “authority domain”. It can be read beside REG. It is not a chapter of REG, and adapters toward TENIR-Gov are a later possibility there, not a definition.</p>

<p>Those two documents are not in the public repositories cloned for this site. They are cited because they were provided as authorial sources.</p>
