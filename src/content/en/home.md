## Diagnostic of an agent’s controls

<p>An action was sent, but its result is still uncertain? Or is your team preparing to put it into service? We examine its rules, its traces, and its conditions for resuming, with you. You leave with a sourced sheet: established facts, open questions, and the next control to check.</p>

<p>One action · one agreed scope · an accompanied restitution.</p>
