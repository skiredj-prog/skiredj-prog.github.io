# KB5-A - Extension: Governed Incarnation & Temporal Architecture

**Status:** future-state doctrine / architectural extension
**Role:** Defines the ontological capabilities and temporal invariants that govern the evolution of the R5 estate.

## 1. R5 as Governed Incarnation
R5 is not a static technology stack; it **operationalizes** Capability-Governed Incarnation. It serves as the first implementation candidate for the system's ability to adapt its form dynamically under strict governance without losing its invariant identity ($TAU$). 

## 2. Temporal Architecture: The Continuity Membrane
The traditional synchronous execution model (decision → adjudication → action) forces all operational, forensic, and ritual processes onto a single timeline. 

R5 resolves this via a **Temporal Continuity Membrane** (continuity-preserving temporal decoupling). 

### 2.1 Decoupled Timelines
The objective is not mere asynchronous processing; the objective is to preserve the continuity of the core kernel while allowing secondary trajectories to evolve on independent axes:
*   **The Decision Timeline:** The synchronous, high-velocity axis where immediate trajectory is preserved.
*   **The Forensic Timeline:** The decoupled axis where historical state replays occur without inducing latency on the decision kernel.

*(Note: Specific execution primitives—such as temporal drift thresholds and state committal mechanics—will be governed under a future `KB5-C: Operational Runtime Doctrine` to separate ontological invariants from runtime engineering).*
