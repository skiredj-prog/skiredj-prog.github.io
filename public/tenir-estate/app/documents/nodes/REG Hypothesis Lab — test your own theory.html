REG HYPOTHESIS LAB

Bring a theory.
Try to break it.

State your claim, write it as a small state machine, and the lab walks every reachable state. You get a verdict, or the shortest sequence of events that proves your own theory wrong.

How to read a verdict. PASS means no reachable state of this finite model violates your invariant. It is not a proof about the real world, and the model is only as faithful as you made it. VACUOUS means the invariant held only because its premise (the left side of =>) was never reached. INCONCLUSIVE means the search hit its state cap and counts as nothing.
