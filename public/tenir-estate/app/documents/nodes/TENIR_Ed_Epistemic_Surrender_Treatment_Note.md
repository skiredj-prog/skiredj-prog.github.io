# E_d / Epistemic Surrender Treatment Note

## Decision
TENIR 2.0 does **not** integrate the former v3 scalar **E_d** into the stability formula.

## Retained structural formula
S = K / (P × V + ε)

## Why E_d is not in S
Embedding E_d multiplicatively inside S would fuse:
- structural strain measurement, and
- epistemic quality / delegation risk

These are related but not identical. TENIR 2.0 keeps them distinct to preserve interpretive clarity.

## Where the substance went
The substantive concern captured by E_d survives in TENIR 2.0 as:
- epistemic sovereignty assessment,
- epistemic_surrender_risk collapse flag,
- posture modifiers,
- irreversible-action gating logic.

## Result
The framework preserves the insight of v3 without contaminating the structural meaning of stability.
