# TENIR 2.0 Posture Determination Protocol

## Purpose
Operationalize posture logic for implementation.

## Constitutional posture states
- NORMAL
- CAUTION
- HOLDING-FIRST

## Decision sequence
1. Validate schema completeness
2. Evaluate epistemic gate
3. Evaluate TAU proximity / breach
4. Evaluate option erosion
5. Evaluate agency failure / coordination risk
6. Evaluate trajectory lock-in / time-to-closure
7. Evaluate profile-specific collapse flags
8. Determine posture

## Minimum threshold definitions
### High epistemic surrender risk
Raised when all are true:
- synthetic or externally mediated epistemics materially shape the asserted reality basis,
- accountable human verification of relevant raw substrate is missing or merely ceremonial,
- the pathway under consideration contains irreversible or high-cost commitments.

### Material effect on an irreversible pathway
A condition materially affects an irreversible pathway when it can alter, authorize, accelerate, or normalize:
- irreversible acts,
- difficult-to-reverse commitments,
- hard external consequences,
- legally, financially, or socially consequential actions.

## Mandatory HOLDING-FIRST triggers
- epistemic status invalid
- TAU violated or silently breached with high probability
- agency failure
- trajectory lock-in
- critical option erosion
- high epistemic surrender risk on an irreversible pathway

## CAUTION triggers
- epistemic status contested
- TAU proximity rising
- agency modifier cautionary
- trajectory modifier cautionary
- narrowing intervention bandwidth

## NORMAL threshold
Permitted only when no HOLDING-FIRST trigger is active and no CAUTION trigger remains materially unresolved.
